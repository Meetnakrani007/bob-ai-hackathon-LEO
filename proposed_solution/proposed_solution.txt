2. PROPOSED SOLUTION

SupplyGuard AI replaces manual, reactive firefighting with an explainable, human-approved decision loop:

- Verify: cross-check disruption reports against multiple sources and assign a confidence score, instead of acting on rumor.
- Identify impact: automatically find every shipment, port, and fleet asset affected by the disruption.
- Protect cold-chain cargo: monitor temperature telemetry in real time and flag excursions before spoilage occurs.
- Compare options: generate 2–3 ranked alternative plans (reroute, redeploy fleet, hold) with cost/time/risk trade-offs.
- Human approval: no action executes automatically; a Logistics Manager or Admin must approve it.
- Audit: every approved action is permanently logged for compliance and accountability.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE 6 CORE CAPABILITIES IN DETAIL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Evidence-Backed Incident Verification
   - Cross-checks Port Authority Emergency Bulletins, Reuters / Lloyd's List, and AIS satellite telemetry.
   - Calculates a quantitative Confidence Score (e.g. 94% verified authenticity) before raising alarms.

2. Automated Impact Identification & Dynamic Risk Studio
   - Scans vessels in the disruption radius and computes a multi-factor risk score (0-100).
   - Embedded Risk Studio allows applying 3 micro-mitigation plans that recalculate risk dynamically.

3. Real-Time Cold-Chain Guard & Fleet Mobilization
   - Monitors container internal temperature (4.2°C) vs. ambient heat (34°C).
   - Triggers one-click Reefer Truck Intercept: mobilizes TRK-REEFER-01 over Atal Setu MTHL corridor.

4. Multi-Factor Scenario Trade-Off Matrix
   - Plan A (JNPT Diversion): 8km distance, 1,450 plugs, saves $1,248,550 USD, post-risk 18/100 (96% Feasible).
   - Plan B (Mundra Rail): 310 NM north, automated rail link, post-risk 34/100 (84% Feasible).
   - Plan C (Hold Anchorage): Stationary idling, 100% vaccine spoilage failure, post-risk 96/100 (Catastrophic).

5. Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Governance Gate
   - Port diversions and fleet mobilizations require authenticated approval by Logistics Manager or Admin.
   - Normal users have read-only visibility.

6. Permanent Cryptographic Audit Trail
   - Every approved intervention generates an immutable SHA-256 cryptographic audit record.
   - Logs timestamp, actor identity, action type, justification, and digital signature hash.
