# 📝 Problem Statement

## Track L2 — Supply Chain Disruption Assistant & Fleet Utilisation Optimizer

Global maritime and intermodal supply chains are critically fragile. When a disruption strikes a major node — a dockworkers' strike, a chokepoint blockade, a reefer power failure — logistics teams face five compounding failures:

- **Information overload** — conflicting reports from ports, news, and telemetry take hours to verify manually.
- **Cold-chain spoilage** — temperature-sensitive cargo (vaccines, pharma) has only a few hours of battery buffer once disconnected from shore power; a single missed excursion can destroy $500K–$1.25M+ in cargo.
- **Demurrage cascades** — a single halted berth generates tens of thousands of dollars per day in penalties and backs up feeder routes.
- **Idle fleet, no visibility** — trucks, vessels, and rail capacity sit idle nearby while other routes overload, because no one has synchronized visibility.
- **Decision paralysis with no audit trail** — trade-off decisions (cost vs. time vs. spoilage risk) get made under pressure via chat/email, with no accountable record.

**Who this affects:** logistics managers, operations analysts, and fleet dispatchers at freight/shipping companies managing hundreds of active shipments across ports and carriers.

### Key Benchmarks
- A single major port disruption affects **$200M+ in cargo** within 48 hours.
- **15–20% of pharmaceutical shipments** experience temperature excursions during disruptions.
