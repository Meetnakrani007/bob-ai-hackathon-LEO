1. PROBLEM STATEMENT

Global maritime and intermodal supply chains are critically fragile. When a disruption strikes a major node — a dockworkers' strike, a chokepoint blockade, a reefer power failure — logistics teams face five compounding failures:

- Information overload: conflicting reports from ports, news, and telemetry take hours to verify manually.
- Cold-chain spoilage: temperature-sensitive cargo (vaccines, pharma) has only a few hours of battery buffer once disconnected from shore power; a single missed excursion can destroy $500K–$1.25M+ in cargo.
- Demurrage cascades: a single halted berth generates tens of thousands of dollars per day in penalties and backs up feeder routes.
- Idle fleet, no visibility: trucks, vessels, and rail capacity sit idle nearby while other routes overload, because no one has synchronized visibility.
- Decision paralysis with no audit trail: trade-off decisions (cost vs. time vs. spoilage risk) get made under pressure via chat/email, with no accountable record.

Who this affects: logistics managers, operations analysts, and fleet dispatchers at freight/shipping companies managing hundreds of active shipments across ports and carriers.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHALLENGE BREAKDOWN MATRIX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Incident Verification
   - Current Industry Reality: Fragmented news wires, conflicting port reports
   - Consequence: 4–8 hour verification latency

2. Cold-Chain Vulnerability
   - Current Industry Reality: Disconnected IoT sensors, delayed thermal alerts
   - Consequence: Vaccine & pharma spoilage ($500K–$1.25M+ loss per incident)

3. Demurrage Accumulation
   - Current Industry Reality: Single halted berths backing up feeder networks
   - Consequence: Tens of thousands of dollars/day in compounding penalties

4. Fleet Asset Coordination
   - Current Industry Reality: Siloed truck/rail dispatchers without shared visibility
   - Consequence: Idle trucks and railcars while cargo perishes

5. Governance & Audit Trail
   - Current Industry Reality: Decisions made under pressure via chat/email
   - Consequence: Zero accountability, insurance claim denials, compliance failures

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
QUANTITATIVE IMPACT BENCHMARKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- A single major port disruption can expose $200M+ in cargo within 48 hours.
- 15% to 20% of sensitive pharmaceutical shipments suffer thermal excursions during port congestion.
- Manual disruption response takes 4 to 8 hours; automated AI response cuts this to under 15 seconds.
